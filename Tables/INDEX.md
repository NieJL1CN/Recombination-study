# Tables

`manuscript/` holds the tables that appear in the manuscript and its
Supplementary Information, numbered as in the paper. `analysis/` holds the
intermediate tables produced by the scripts, with their original working names
(`Table22c_...`, `Table178_...`); these are inputs to figures and are not
numbered in the paper.
